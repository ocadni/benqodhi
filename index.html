BENchmarking Quantum Optimization: from Datasets to Health Innovation

BENQODHI brings together optimisation, biology, health, AI and quantum-computing experts to define open benchmarks for problems that matter in life sciences and health. The goal is fair comparison across classical, AI-based, hybrid and quantum approaches.

BENQODHI is a European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC) exploratory activity. It is shaped around a simple question: which health and biology optimization problems are specific, measurable and useful enough to become shared benchmarks?

Three Commitments

01

Focus

Make the questions real. Optimization challenges rooted in health and biology — not generic combinatorial problems dressed in biological vocabulary.

02

Method

Make comparisons fair. Instances, baselines, metrics and resource accounting that let classical, AI-based, hybrid and quantum methods be compared on equal terms.

03

Outcome

Make evidence reusable. A practical pathway toward open benchmark resources the community can build on.

Origin: the BENQODHI workshop

The project’s first milestone is the BENQODHI Workshop, 10–11 September 2026 at JRC Ispra, Italy. The workshop convenes invited speakers and contributors to sketch a first set of candidate benchmark problems and a shared framework for building them out.
